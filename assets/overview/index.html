01 / Origins

Born in a garage. Built for everyone. A search box that became the world's front door.

Larry Page / Sergey Brin
Stanford University, 1996;
incorporated September 4, 1998.

Larry Page and Sergey Brin met at Stanford University in 1995. Their research project, BackRub, analyzed web link structures to rank pages by relevance. Incorporated in 1998, Google quickly became the world's most trusted search engine.

Founded by two Stanford PhD students The PageRank breakthrough.

Larry Page and Sergey Brin devised an algorithm that ranked pages by the quality and quantity of links pointing to them.

09 / Principles Ten things · Google believes
10.
Principle 01 / Users First

Focus on the user.

All else will follow. Google's first principle: serve the user's needs without compromise, and commercial success follows naturally.

Principle 02 / Depth

It's best to do one thing well.

Google started with search and mastered it before expanding. Deep expertise in a problem beats shallow coverage of many problems.

Principle 03 / Speed

Fast is better than slow.

Every millisecond matters. Google optimizes every layer of its stack to minimize latency and keep information flowing without friction.

Principle 04 / Democracy

Democracy of the web works.

PageRank was built on the insight that the web's link structure is a collective vote — democracy in hypertext form — that surfaces truth.
